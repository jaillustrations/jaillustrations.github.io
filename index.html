WHO I AM

I'm a travelling artist who reconnected with drawing during my travels across South America, Asia and Australia. Along the way, I developed a fine-line style, bringing my experiences, dreams and adventures to life on paper.

WHAT INSPIRES ME

Sunsets, animals, forests, the underwater world, exploring new countries and discovering new cultures. Hiking mountains, sleeping in a tent, looking at the stars, diving into the ocean, climbing, playing football, and reading good books.

Music, inspiring stories, deep conversations and stupid jokes. A good laugh with friends, and people who dare to follow their dreams and build a different life that feels true to them.
