Hey, I'm Juan.

Artist, traveller and nature enthusiast from Spain.

I'm Juan, from Spain. I'm currently based in Fremantle, Western Australia, where I've been living for the past couple of years.

Drawing was one of my passions as a kid, but it wasn't until many years later, when I started travelling around the world, that I reconnected with it.

I've travelled through South America, Asia, Europe and Australia. Along the way, with just a backpack carrying everything I needed, I've had countless amazing experiences: meeting people from completely different backgrounds, discovering new cultures and ways of seeing the world, exploring wonderful natural landscapes, volunteering on different projects and making lots of new friends.

All these adventures and experiences naturally inspired me to express myself through drawing, bringing them to life on paper.

I've developed a fine-line style using black ink, with occasional touches of colour — most often an orange sunset.

Movement and change are two driving forces that inspire me to keep creating.
